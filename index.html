OUR CULINARY CODE

Why We Don't Take Shortcuts in Patna

Authentic Earthen Ahuna

We source hand-thrown clay pots directly from local Champaran potters. Each pot is used once, sealed with wheat dough, and slow-stewed on sal wood charcoal.

No Frozen Meats or Gravy Bases

Unlike commercial chain restaurants with pre-frozen gravy buckets, our curries are tempered fresh twice a day. Fresh spices are ground in-house every morning.

Warm Bihari Mehmaan-Nawazi

Hospitality in Patna is an art form. We welcome every family with genuine warmth, honoring special dietary customs, and treating every meal like an intimate family celebration.
