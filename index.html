Our table

Not a restaurant. A carnival of the old kitchens.

Food Carnival began on Bailey Road with one clay oven and a stubborn belief that real flavour cannot be hurried. The handi is sealed with dough. The charcoal is fed by hand. The kebab is judged by scent, not by a timer.

Seven years on, the tandoor still burns wood, the dal still simmers eighteen hours, and the same Ustad still refuses to check a thermometer. Come hungry — leave with the smoke of it on your clothes.

  • Wood-fired clay tandoor, never gas
  • Champaran-style ahuna mutton, handi-sealed
  • FSSAI 5-star kitchen · seating & takeaway
