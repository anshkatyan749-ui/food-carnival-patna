Our table

Not a restaurant. A carnival of the old kitchens.

Food Carnival began in Patna, in 2018 with one clay oven and a stubborn belief that real flavour cannot be hurried. The handi is sealed with dough. The charcoal is fed by hand. The kebab is judged by scent, not by a timer.

Eight years on, the tandoor still burns wood, the dal still simmers eighteen hours, and the same Ustad still refuses to check a thermometer. Come hungry — leave with the smoke of it on your clothes.

  • Wood-fired clay tandoor, never gas
  • Champaran-style ahuna mutton, handi-sealed
  • FSSAI 5-star kitchen · seating & takeaway

About us

Feeding Patna the slow way, since 2018.

We are a family kitchen at A.V.S. Complex, Agamkuan — a few steps from S2 Mall — where the fire is lit by hand every morning at half past eleven.

North Indian classics and dum biryani share the pass with wok-fired Indo-Chinese and continental comfort plates, because a carnival belongs to everyone. Everything is cooked to order: no pre-cooked gravies, no gas tandoor, no shortcuts.
